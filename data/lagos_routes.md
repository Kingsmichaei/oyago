# OyaGo Lagos Route Knowledge

The routes below are practical Lagos public-transport routes. Fares are approximate/current guide values and may change with fuel prices, traffic, route changes, or transport operators. Verify firsthand before treating a route as trusted knowledge.

## Oshodi To Abule-Egba

**Mode:** BRT

**Steps:**

1. Board the BRT at Oshodi Transport Interchange. Ask for an Abule-Egba BRT.
2. Stay on the BRT through the Agege corridor and get down at Abule-Egba Terminal.
3. If your final destination is farther inside Abule-Egba, connect with a keke or danfo from the terminal.

**Fare:** ₦680 BRT

**Time:** About 35 min – 1 hr, depending on traffic

**Tips:** Use the official BRT terminal rather than entering an unofficial bus. Confirm the destination before boarding.

**Also known as:** Oshodi, Oshodi Interchange, Abule-Egba, Abule Egba

# Route Knowledge Rules

1. Never claim a route is personally verified unless a trusted traveler has actually used the route.
2. Fares are estimates unless they come directly from the current LAMATA fare table.
3. Danfo fares can vary significantly from official regulated-route fares.
4. Always confirm the destination with the conductor before boarding.
5. Lagos traffic can make travel time highly unpredictable.
6. Rush-hour periods generally require significantly more travel time.
7. Carry small denominations of naira when using danfo or keke.
8. For BRT, check the current fare and payment requirements before travelling.
9. When a route has multiple possible terminals or stops, the AI should ask for the passenger's exact destination rather than assuming the final stop.
10. The AI should never invent a conductor's exact wording, bus-stop landmark, or fare when the information has not been verified.
11. When route information conflicts, prefer current LAMATA information for official BRT/standard routes and firsthand/community-verified information for informal danfo/keke routes.
12. Fares and routes should be periodically reviewed because Lagos transport prices and routes change.
